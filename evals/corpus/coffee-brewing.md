# Coffee Brewing Guide

## Water

Use water between 90 and 96 degrees Celsius. Boiling water extracts bitter compounds and makes the cup harsh. Filtered water with moderate mineral content gives the most balanced flavour.

## Ratio

A good starting ratio is 1 gram of coffee to 16 grams of water. For a stronger cup, move towards 1 to 15. Weigh both the coffee and the water rather than measuring by volume.

## Grind size

Espresso needs a fine grind, close to powdered sugar. Pour-over works best with a medium-fine grind similar to table salt. French press needs a coarse grind so the metal filter can hold it back.

## Brew time

A pour-over should finish in about three minutes. A French press steeps for four minutes before plunging. Espresso shots run for 25 to 30 seconds.

## Storage

Store beans in an airtight container away from light, heat and moisture. Whole beans stay fresh for about a month after roasting. Do not keep coffee in the fridge, where it absorbs odours and condensation.
