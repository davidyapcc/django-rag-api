# Sourdough Basics

## Starter

A sourdough starter is a mix of flour and water colonised by wild yeast and lactic acid bacteria. Feed it equal weights of flour and water once a day when kept at room temperature. A healthy starter doubles in size within four to eight hours of feeding.

## Hydration

Hydration is the weight of water divided by the weight of flour. A beginner dough at 70 percent hydration is easier to shape. Higher hydration, around 80 percent, gives a more open crumb but is stickier to handle.

## Bulk fermentation

Bulk fermentation usually takes four to six hours at 24 degrees Celsius. Perform a set of stretch and folds every 30 minutes during the first two hours to build gluten strength.

## Baking

Bake in a preheated Dutch oven at 250 degrees Celsius for 20 minutes with the lid on, then remove the lid and bake another 20 to 25 minutes. The trapped steam lets the loaf expand before the crust sets.

## Cooling

Let the loaf cool for at least one hour before slicing. The crumb is still setting as steam escapes, and cutting early makes it gummy.
